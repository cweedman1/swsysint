2035 02702 Double Suspension Load Hook Assembly
Capacity 150 lbs per Assembly.
By suspending the load from two points on the chain, the load carrying capacity is increased. This pendant can be attached to the conveyor chain on a minimum of 12"centers. The Load Hook assembly is used for finishing applications where the load must be manually indexed in 90° increments.
